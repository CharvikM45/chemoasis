Privacy notice

Your information, in plain words.

Chemoasis is a student-built project. Here is everything it keeps about you, why, and how to remove it.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email, the name you choose, and your password stored only as a secure scramble (we can't read it).
  • Your questionnaire answers: things like treatment status and what you'd like to work on. This is health information, so it is only used to suggest games to you.
  • Your games: which games you play, levels you finish and your scores, so you can see your progress.
  • Your community posts: anything you post in the community can be read by other people who have a Chemoasis account. It isn't public on the web. Your forum name starts as "Member" and a number, and you choose what to change it to. If you post anonymously, your name isn't shown, but moderators can still see who wrote it.

Who can see it

Only you can see your answers, suggestions and scores. They are never shown in the community and never sold or shared. Your community posts are seen by other members, as described above. The student team that runs Chemoasis can access the database to keep it working.

Where it's stored

On a secure database (Postgres, hosted by Railway). Chemoasis is not a medical service and is not covered by health privacy laws like HIPAA, so please only share what you're comfortable with. Every question can be skipped.

Deleting everything

Sign in, open Your games, and choose Delete my account and data. Your account, answers, scores and settings are deleted straight away, and your community posts are taken down and their words erased.

Children

If you're under 13, please ask a parent or guardian to set up your account with you and to read this notice.

Not medical advice

Chemoasis's games are inspired by tasks used in published research, but they have not been studied themselves or clinically reviewed and are not a treatment. For new, severe or worsening symptoms, contact your care team. If you're thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 in the US, or your local emergency number.
