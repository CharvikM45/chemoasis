Community guidelines

How we look after each other

This forum is for people recovering after chemotherapy, and for the people who support them. It's a place to share experiences and ideas. It is not a place for diagnosis or medical treatment.

If you need help now

  • For new, severe or worsening symptoms, contact your care team. They know your situation.
  • In an emergency, call 911.
  • If you are thinking about suicide or are in emotional crisis, call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US).

The guidelines

  1. Be kind. Recovery looks different for everyone. Disagree gently, and never mock or pressure anyone.
  2. Share experiences, not prescriptions. "This helped me" is welcome. Telling someone to start, stop or change a treatment or medication is not.
  3. Label your post. Every post is marked as a personal experience, general information, or professional guidance, so readers know what they're reading.
  4. Back up information. If you share a fact, add a link to where it came from, like a cancer center or government health page.
  5. Protect privacy. Share only what you're comfortable with. Never post someone else's health details, contact information or photos without permission.
  6. No selling. No ads, supplements, fundraising links or spam.

What the labels mean

Personal experience
Something that happened to the person posting. It may not apply to you.
General information
Educational information, ideally with a source you can check.
Professional guidance
General guidance from a verified professional. It isn't advice about your own care. Your care team is the right place for that.

What the badges mean

Verified professional
We checked this person's professional credentials before giving the badge. The badge confirms those credentials, not every statement the account makes.
Activity creator
This person made or contributed an activity in the library. Being a creator is not a medical qualification.
Moderator
Moderators review reports and keep the community safe.

Talking about games

Tell others how an activity felt: how hard it was, whether you enjoyed it, and whether it worked with your accessibility needs. Popularity, ratings and "found helpful" counts show what people liked. They are not proof that a game improves any medical condition.

Tools that help you take part your way

  • Edit fixes a typo or adds a detail to your post. Edited posts say "(edited)", and edits go through the same safety check as new posts.
  • Saved drafts keep a post you haven't sent yet in this browser, so a tired moment or a closed tab doesn't lose it.
  • Listen reads a post aloud using your device's built-in voice.
  • Mute on someone's profile hides their posts from you only. They aren't told. Anonymous posts and moderators can't be muted.
  • Larger text and post anonymously by default are on your profile page.

Reporting and moderation

  • Use Report on any post that breaks these guidelines. Reports are private.
  • A post that may contain an unsafe medical claim is held for a moderator to review before others can see it.
  • If several different people report the same post, it is hidden until a moderator reviews it.
  • Moderators can keep, hide or remove a post. If you think a decision about your post was wrong, use Ask for another review on it once, and a moderator will look again.
