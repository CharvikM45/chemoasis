Before you publish

  • The credentials belong to this person. If a registry didn't match, confirm another way (an institution web page, or their institution's email address).
  • The studies exist and support what the listing says, for people during or after chemotherapy. Read the abstracts.
  • No promises to treat, cure or reverse anything. Small or mixed results are described as such.
  • Gentle by default: no forced timer, a pause at any moment, short rounds.
  • Accessible: big targets, nothing that relies on colour alone, no required dragging, calm motion.
  • Private: it doesn't ask players for personal or health information.

Chemoasis is a supportive platform, not a diagnostic tool or a replacement for oncology, rehabilitation or other clinical care. For new, severe or worsening symptoms, contact your care team. In a crisis, call or text 988 in the US, or your local emergency number.
