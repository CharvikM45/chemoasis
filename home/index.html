Take some control back

Chemo runs on everyone else's schedule. This part runs on yours.

During treatment, your days are shaped by appointments, infusions and side effects. Chemoasis gives you something you can do for yourself, starting now, whether you're in chemo or finished.

You don't have to wait

Thinking and memory changes often show up during treatment, not only after it ends. So Chemoasis is made for both: practise focus and memory on the days you feel up to it, and keep going in the months after, as you work toward feeling like yourself again.

Our approach

Focus, brain retraining and community, in one balance.

Treatment days are unpredictable. Chemoasis turns brain retraining into short activities you can finish on a good day or a hard one, picked for how you're actually doing.

  1. 01
    Tell us how you're doing

    Ten short questions about your treatment, your energy and what feels hardest right now. Skip any of them.

  2. 02
    Get three games to start with

    Ranked for you, each with the reasons it was picked, so nothing is a black box.

  3. 03
    Train, a few minutes at a time

    Each game is inspired by exercises studied with people after cancer treatment (our versions haven't been tested themselves), and adapts as you play: harder when you're doing well, easier when you're not.

  4. 04
    It learns with you

    Every game you play, or set aside, tunes your next suggestions. Your progress is saved to your account.

Research from experts anywhere

Brain science keeps moving. Our Open Research Studio, now in preview, lets researchers and clinicians anywhere share activities based on their work, with their names on them, after a human review. That means research from experts anywhere, not just where you're treated. The community is there for the part games can't do: talking with people who've been through it.
