Why it matters

Research from experts anywhere, not just where you're treated.

Chemo brain can start during treatment, not only after it (National Cancer Institute ↗). Your work can help patients take some control back, wherever they are.

In the library

Built on published research.

    How it works

    From a study to the library.

    1. 01

      Sketch it

      Describe the activity. The design assistant draws a mock-up.

    2. 02

      Show the evidence

      Link studies by DOI or PubMed, and say what they found and didn't.

    3. 03

      Get checked

      Your credentials and every study are checked against public registries.

    4. 04

      A person decides

      A reviewer publishes it with your credit. Software never approves.

    Every activity is

    • Honest. It claims only what the research shows, and never to treat a condition.
    • Gentle. No forced timer, a pause at any moment, short rounds.
    • Accessible. Big targets, no colour-only cues, no required dragging.
    • Private. It runs sealed off from accounts and never collects personal or health information.

    The fine print

    Free, and yours to keep.

    What do I agree to?

    One checkbox, five promises: you made it or may share it; your credentials and research are real; we may host it free and adjust it for accessibility or safety, always crediting you; you keep ownership and can take it down at any time; it collects no personal or health information. This agreement is in preview and will be reviewed again before we invite researchers widely.

    Does it cost anything?

    No. Submitting and publishing are free, and players never pay to play.

    Is publishing the same as running a study?

    No. Any research use of how people play would need their consent, proper oversight and ethics review first.

    What if it doesn't pass?

    You'll get the reasons, and you can change it and send it again.

    Want to be one of the first?

    Create a free account, start an activity, and send it for review when it's ready.
